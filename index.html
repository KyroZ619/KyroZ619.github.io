ABOUT ME

about kyro ✎

Kyro Zhao is a Music Business student at Berklee College of Music with interests in artist branding, music marketing, and creative strategy. He is passionate about helping artists build distinctive and authentic identities by connecting business thinking with visual storytelling, and has worked on release planning and social media campaigns that translate an artist’s personality into a clear and recognizable public image.

Alongside his work in music business, Kyro is a dancer, electronic music producer, and vocalist. His experience as both a creator and strategist gives him a firsthand understanding of the artist perspective and shapes the way he approaches creative decisions, audience connection, and long-term artist development.

Having lived and studied across Asia, the United States, and Europe, Kyro brings a cross-cultural perspective to how he thinks about audiences and music markets. He is also exploring artist rights, attribution, and creative ownership in the AI era. Ultimately, Kyro hopes to help distinctive creative voices build identities that can be seen, understood, and remembered.

Explore Kyro’s selected work to see how he brings creative thinking and music business strategy together in practice.

— K.
